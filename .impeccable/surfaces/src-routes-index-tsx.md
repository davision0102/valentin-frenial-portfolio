# Home and project pages: Valentin Frenial portfolio

Scope: home (`/`) and project pages (`/projets/<slug>`). Visitor mode: Experience (the work leads).
Audience: recruiters / learning managers and freelance clients, from LinkedIn or a CV, desktop and phone.
Constraints: texts and projects from his PDF stay as they are; project carousels are binding; FR/EN and dark theme kept; no "template" look, nothing "scolaire"; he is a digital learning project manager, not a video editor (the editing-timeline direction was rejected by the user).

## Direction contract

THESIS: A modern Apple-style portfolio: big centered statement, then large project cards in horizontal carousels, like apple.com's product carousels. Refuses the video-editing metaphor and the starter-template landing page.

OWN-WORLD: Apple glass (pinned): floating frosted header pill, glass buttons and chips, ambient backdrop made from a project visual, blurred and saturated. Solid rounded cards for content, glass only for chrome. Category colours: indigo for e-learning, orange for design, green for skills. Geist display tight-tracked, Inter body.

STORY: The visitor reads who he is in one line, sees three key figures, swipes through e-learning then design projects, opens one, and contacts him at the end.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass header pill. Centered: "Bonjour, moi c'est Valentin." with the role in grey below, pitch, "Voir mes projets" and "Me contacter", then three glass chips with real figures. Ambient colour from the first project behind.
