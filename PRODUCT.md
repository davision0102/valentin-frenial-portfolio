# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, equally important:

- **Recruiters and HR / learning managers** hiring a digital learning project manager (CDI). They skim many portfolios; they need to understand in seconds what Valentin does, for whom, and see real work.
- **Freelance clients**: companies or agencies that need a training programme produced (e-learning, video, onboarding). They want proof he can deliver, on time, with craft.

Both arrive mostly from LinkedIn or a CV link, on desktop and phone.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Valentin Frenial, "chef de projet digital e-learning". It shows his projects and gets visitors to contact him (email, LinkedIn). Success: a visitor opens a project, sees the work, and reaches out.

## Positioning

He sits between learning design and graphic design: he scripts e-learning, builds interactions, does art direction, shoots and edits video and motion design, and runs the project. Proof comes from real projects for Nexans and Chanel (work-study at Chanel).

## Operating Context

Visitors compare him with other learning designers and project managers. Projects are visual (screens of e-learning modules, posters, videos, branding mock-ups) and are shown as image carousels.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19 + Vite + Tailwind 4 + TanStack Router, deployed on GitHub Pages (subpath `/valentin-frenial-portfolio/`).
- Bilingual FR / EN (all copy in `src/i18n/`), light and dark theme.
- Home (intro, projects, skills, about, contact) and one page per project (`/projets/<slug>`).
- Carousels for projects are a binding requirement (he likes them).

## Brand Commitments

- Texts and projects come from his own PDF portfolio and must stay as they are.
- Must not look like a template or a product landing page, and must not feel "scolaire" (like a training deck).
- Previous sage-green look is not binding.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six projects with real visuals cropped from his PDF in `public/projets/`: Compliance Week 2024 (Nexans, 6 modules, 12 languages), Climate Training (Nexans, part 2; part 1 had 1,700+ learners), Onboarding Artisans (Chanel), Kit Chanel 25 (Chanel, 6 modules in 1 month, confidential), La Minute Métier (Nexans, 14 videos, LinkedIn trailer), Nymphe Essentials (final-year branding project).
- Absent, must not be invented: photo of Valentin, email and LinkedIn URL (placeholders), project years except 2024, testimonials, client logos files.

## Product Principles

- The work leads: visuals first, words support.
- Readable in seconds by a recruiter, explorable by a curious client.
- Personality without noise: craft over effects.
